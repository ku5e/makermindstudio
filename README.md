# MakerMindStudio

Digital marketplace for parametric 3D models and custom laser designs.

**Shop:** [makermindstudio.com](https://makermindstudio.com)

## About

MakerMindStudio offers high-quality, customizable 3D printable models and laser engraving designs for makers, educators, and small businesses.

### Product Categories
- Parametric desk organizers and nameplates
- Functional household items
- Educational tools and classroom aids
- Custom laser designs (wood and metal)

### Design Philosophy
- Parametric designs (fully customizable)
- Functional and practical
- Easy to print/manufacture
- Professional quality

## Tech Stack
- Design: Fusion360
- Manufacturing: 3D printing (FDM & Resin), Laser engraving (Diode & Fiber)
- Shop Platform: Payhip
- Website: GitHub Pages (Publii)
- Domain: makermindstudio.com

## Equipment
- Creality K1C & K2 Combo (FDM 3D printers)

- Falcon 5W & Daja DJ6 3W Fiber (Laser engravers)
